# Research Interests
I'm broadly interested in making neural networks simulate physics well
    [<a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=YQVoOjgAAAAJ&hl=en">Google Scholar</a>]. I'm currently thinking about how to build data-driven surrogate models for complex physics that:

[+] produce useful forecasts

[+] enable rapid iteration for engineering design

[+] accelerate scientific experiments and inverse problems
