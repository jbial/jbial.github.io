Research Interests

I'm broadly interested in making neural networks simulate physics well [Google Scholar]. I'm currently thinking about how to build data-driven surrogate models for complex physics that:

[+] produce useful forecasts

[+] enable rapid iteration for engineering design

[+] accelerate scientific experiments and inverse problems
